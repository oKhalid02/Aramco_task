## Summary

- **H1 (development set):** 58/58 erroneous invoices flagged, 0 false alarms, all 18 error categories
  matched, expected total exact on 909/913. H1 was used to build the pipeline, so treat this as an upper
  bound. The first run, before any H1-driven fix, already flagged 58/58 with 0 false alarms.
- **Stress test on all five hospitals (2,040 planted errors):** 2,024 flagged (99.2%). Every one of the
  18 standard error types plus the H3 amendment and H5 multiplier traps was flagged with the right
  category (1,840/1,840). All 16 misses came from the two deliberately hard types, and all 16 landed in
  the human-review queue.
- **Four systematic weaknesses** (below): look-alike service descriptions, a wrong unit hiding a price
  error, knock-on findings from a broken line, and daily-cap amounts where the labels follow a different
  convention from the contract.

## 3. Failure types

### A. Look-alike descriptions of services not in the contract
**Mechanism.** Descriptions routinely drop words, so the matcher accepts a service whose other words
fit. A non-contract service that differs only in the dropped word then looks like a contract service.
**Example.** Planted `Supervised Dialysis Session` (INV-H1-000308) matched a contracted dialysis service
and was flagged as `unit_price_mismatch`: right flag, wrong category and amount. `Standard Dialysis
Session` (INV-H1-000751) tied between two services, was left unpriced, and went to review at 0.70. In
real H1 data, INV-H1-000236 was caught only by the rule "loose match whose unit and price fit nothing".
**Size.** Over 500 look-alike plants (5 seeds): 76.2% found as unknown service, 12.8% flagged under
another category, 9.8% sent to review, **1.2% missed silently at high confidence.** In the submission,
the LLM text-only second opinion agreed with every non-exact match in H2–H5.

### B. A wrong unit hides a price error on the same line
**Mechanism.** A line billed in the wrong unit cannot be priced, so its price is never checked.
**Example.** INV-H1-000645: unit changed to `per_procedure` and price doubled. Only `wrong_unit_basis`
is reported, and the expected total keeps the inflated line (2,148,910 cents vs the true 1,936,535).
**Size.** 0/100 planted price errors reported; 22/100 turned into `unknown_service`. **54 submitted
invoices** are flagged `wrong_unit_basis` with the price on that line unchecked.

### C. Knock-on findings from one broken line
**Mechanism.** A line with an unreadable or moved date, or an unknown description, drops out of the
history that other lines depend on: bundle partners, running discount totals.
**Example.** INV-H1-000388: a malformed date on *Advanced Cardiac Recovery Room Occupancy* removes the
bundle for the same-day *Routine Cardiac Specimen Analysis*. Its correct bundled price (191.50) is then
reported as `unit_price_mismatch` against the standalone 233.50, and the expected total rises £294.
**Size.** 37 of 400 date/unknown-service plants gained an extra category, and 24 untouched invoices
changed in those batches. 40 submitted invoices show the pattern (date or unknown finding plus a
price finding on another line). On H1, all 10 invoices with this pattern were genuinely multi-error.

### D. Daily-cap amounts: contract vs label convention
**Mechanism.** The contract makes the excess over the cap unpayable, so we pay cap × rate. H1 labels
pay fewer units than the cap in all four cases (3, 3, 3, 9), consistent with restoring the pre-error
quantity, which is not in the data.
**Example.** INV-H1-000725: 11 dialysis sessions billed, cap 8. We expect 8 × 376.00; the label pays 3.
**Size.** H1 totals 0/4. Stress: 100/100 match the contract and 10/100 the original. **28 submitted
invoices**: flag confidence stays high, amount confidence 0.20, review type "amount uncertain".

## 5. What would reduce each failure type

- **A:** score description matches by how many *distinguishing* words were dropped, and have the LLM
  judge look-alikes with the unit and price visible (a v2 mapping prompt).
- **B:** when the unit is wrong, try pricing under each plausible unit and report a price error if none
  reconciles.
- **C:** keep broken lines in the bundle and discount history by the invoice's admission dates, or mark
  dependent findings as consequential.
- **D:** ask the payer which convention applies. Until then, report both amounts.
